Item, spell, and talent data — for looking something up rather than reading a guide's opinion.

wowhead.com/tbc/talent-calc
The database everything else gets cross-checked against — items, spells, NPCs, and a full TBC talent tree calculator.

classic.warcraftdb.com
Classic-era item/talent/NPC database, including a talent calculator.

Long-running Classic-era item and talent database; a common fallback when Wowhead's TBC filter is inconvenient.

Third-party armory and talent calculators spanning Classic, TBC, and retail.
Royal Giraffe — Classic calculators ↗
royalgiraffe.github.io
